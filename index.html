HOW TO PLAY · CLASSIC BOARD RULES
  • Roll and move forward by the number shown. Your first roll moves you onto the board from just before square 1.
  • Only landing on a ladder's foot climbs you to its top. Only landing on a snake's head slides you to its tail; passing over one does nothing.
  • Reach square 100 by an exact roll. If your move would pass 100, stay where you are and try again next turn.
  • Both players may share a square. Turns pass after every roll, including the highest roll.

Arcade dice handicaps: Easy: you 2–6, computer 1–4. Normal: both 1–6. Hard: you 1–4, computer 2–6. These custom ranges change the dice odds; the board rules stay the same.
